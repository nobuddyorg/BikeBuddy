      How to use BikeBuddy

      🧭 What is this?

      Upload GPX tracks from your rides — cycling or motorcycling — and see them as an interactive heatmap. Warmer colours mean you rode there more often.

      🔑 Sign in & your account

      Sign in to upload tours and save them to your account. Your profile lets you set a display name, switch language, export your data, or delete your account.

      ⬆️ Upload a tour

      Click Upload GPX, give it a name, and drop a .gpx file. It appears on the map straight away.

      🔍 Find your tours

      Use the search box to filter by name and the sort menu to order by date, name, or length. Toggle In view to show only tours visible on the current map.

      🗑️ Select & delete multiple

      Click Select in the sidebar to check off several tours and delete them together.

      🗺️ Map & heatmap

      Click a tour to focus it; Show All Tours brings back the combined heatmap. Warmer colours mean you rode there more often.

      📷 Photos & pins

      Open a tour to add, view, or delete photos. Toggle Photo pins on the map to show geotagged photos where they were taken.

      📱 On mobile

      Swipe a tour right to delete it, or left to open its details.
